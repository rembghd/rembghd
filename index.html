Questions

Is it really free?

Yes — 3 low-resolution removals free every day, no account, no watermark. Full HD is Pro: $12/month for 100 HD images.

Are my images uploaded anywhere?

Free removals run entirely on your device — nothing is uploaded. HD Pro images are sent to our server only to remove the background (Bria RMBG-2.0 via fal.ai) and are not stored.

What quality do I get?

Free gives you a low-resolution PNG (up to 1024 px on the long side). Pro gives you the full-resolution transparent PNG at your original size.

How do I get Pro?

Click “Buy Pro — $12 / month” above and email us. We send you a licence key; paste it in the Pro licence box on this page and HD unlocks on any device.

What do the erase and restore brushes do?

Erase paints away leftover background. Restore paints the subject back where the AI cut too much. Both work on the full-resolution result before you download.
