Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no account, no watermark and no per-image fee. The AI runs in your browser, so it costs us nothing per image — and that saving is yours.

Are my images uploaded anywhere?

No. Your photo is processed entirely on your device. That is also why the first removal takes a little longer: the AI engine (about 40 MB) downloads once and is then cached by your browser.

What quality do I get?

You get a full-resolution transparent PNG at the same size as your original image — ready for products, profiles, documents and designs.
